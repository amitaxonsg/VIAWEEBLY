About VIA Frontiers

VIA Frontiers was founded in Sydney in 2002 and relocated to Singapore in 2007. The organisation specialises in coaching, training, facilitation and consulting throughout Asia Pacific.

Sylvia Fernandes

Sylvia Fernandes launched her business in Sydney in 2002, moving from a career in banking into work as a Behaviour Change Specialist. She is a Licensed Master Trainer of Neuro Linguistic Programming (NLP), recognised by The Society of NLP.

What VIA Frontiers does

Coaching and corporate programmes cover communication, leadership, sales, teamwork and behavioural change.
