Bye Bye Black Cat — Turn Your Luck Around and Realise Opportunities

Author: Sylvia Fernandes
Publisher: VIA Frontiers Pte Ltd
Publication: 18 May 2016
ISBN: 9789810911188
Google Play ebook edition: 221 pages

Book overview

Set against an Australian outback road trip, the story follows twin brothers Jack and Joe as they examine how their ways of thinking influence their experiences and opportunities. The book combines a fictional journey with reflections on personal change and the idea of creating one's own luck.

Structure

The book combines a road-trip story with a separate analysis exploring change and personal development. It is presented as a self-help resource and as material that can support workplace learning workshops.
