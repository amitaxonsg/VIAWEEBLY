Meet Sylvia Fernandes

A Licensed Master Trainer of Neuro Linguistic Programming (NLP) awarded by The Society of NLP, Sylvia launched her business in Sydney, Australia, in 2002. She re-invented herself from a career in banking to a Behaviour Change Specialist and now lives in Singapore.

She was personally endorsed by NLP co-founder Dr. Richard Bandler as a Licensed Master Trainer of NLP, certified by the Society of NLP.

NLP Technology — Accelerate to Success

NLP is an accelerated learning model focused on changing behaviour patterns and building more empowering habits. VIA Frontiers provides corporate training and coaching in leadership, collaboration, sales, communication, presentation and self-management.

Highlighted programmes include Create Your Future Now, NLP Practitioner, NLP Corporate Programs, and Rapid Success Coaching.
