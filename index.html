Meet Sylvia Fernandes

A Licensed Master Trainer of Neuro Linguistic Programming (NLP) awarded by The Society of NLP, Sylvia launched her business in Sydney, Australia, in 2002. She re-invented herself from a career in banking to a Behaviour Change Specialist and now lives in Singapore.

Sylvia was personally endorsed by NLP co-founder Dr. Richard Bandler as a Licensed Master Trainer of NLP, certified by the Society of NLP. An earlier website announced recognition in Orlando, Florida in July 2025.

NLP Technology — Accelerate to Success

VIA Frontiers describes NLP as an accelerated learning model designed to change unhelpful behaviour patterns and build empowering habits. Its training and coaching programmes focus on communication, leadership, self-management, employee engagement and team collaboration.

Programme pathways

  • Create Your Future Now: personal performance and purposeful living.
  • NLP Practitioner: tools for success in life and work.
  • NLP Corporate Programs: self-management, collaboration and leadership.
  • Rapid Success Coaching: focused coaching for faster outcomes.

About VIA Frontiers

VIA Frontiers was founded in Sydney in 2002 and relocated to Singapore in 2007. The organisation specialises in coaching, training, facilitation and consulting throughout Asia Pacific, using Neuro-linguistic Programming and its Rapid Success Technology methodology.

Its original website includes biographies of Sylvia Fernandes and team members, organisational values, culture change programmes, a social responsibility project and client logos.
